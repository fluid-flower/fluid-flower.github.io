Android testing — join in two steps

Google needs at least 12 testers before the app can go public on Google Play. The two steps below are Google's setup, not mine. Takes about a minute.

1

Join the tester group

Sign in with the Google account you use on your phone, then tap Join.

Join the group
2

Become a tester

Open this after step 1 and hit “Become a tester”. The install link shows up on that same page.

Become a tester

Seeing a red “no access” screen on step 1? That's normal — it just means you're not a member yet. Sign in and the Join button appears.

Joined but still blocked? Reload the page. It doesn't refresh itself right after you join.

App not in the Play Store yet? Give it a few hours after step 2 — Google takes a while.

One ask: keep it installed for 14 days and play a bit. That's Google's rule, and it's the last thing standing between the app and the store.
